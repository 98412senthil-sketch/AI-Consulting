# Flagship Case Study
## Enterprise Latent Regime Discovery & Decision Governance Platform

### Problem
Traditional customer analytics clusters customers but does not discover behavioral structure,
leading to fragile segmentation, silent drift, and unclear decision accountability.

### Solution
A probabilistic latent regime discovery platform that:

• Discovers hidden behavioral regimes  
• Models uncertainty explicitly  
• Assigns human accountability to AI decisions  
• Monitors structural drift continuously  

### Architecture Layers

| Layer | Purpose |
|-----|--------|
| GMM Baseline | Initial regime discovery |
| MFA / PPCA | Latent structural behavior modeling |
| Decision Layer | Human-owned policy governance |
| Drift Engine | Continuous structural monitoring |

### Business Impact

| Regime | Decision Outcome |
|------|----------------|
| Dormant Risk | Reactivation & churn mitigation |
| Normal Retail | Retention & product growth |
| High-Value Concentrated | RM assignment & wealth expansion |

This platform transforms AI from prediction engines into **governable decision systems.**
