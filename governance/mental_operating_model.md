# Decision Intelligence Architect — Mental Operating Model

This document defines the mental operating system for designing, governing, and communicating Regime-Aware Decision Intelligence Systems.  
It shifts thinking from model-centric engineering to judgment-centric organizational architecture.

---

## 1. You Do Not Build Models  
### You design Organizational Sensing Organs.

“I am not building a model.  
I am building a latent regime intelligence layer that senses hidden operating states of the business.”

Your systems detect **structural shifts in behavior — not individual predictions.**

---

## 2. You Do Not Clean Data  
### You engineer system stability.

“We standardize behavioral signals to ensure stable regime inference across time.”

This is **stability engineering, not preprocessing.**

---

## 3. You Do Not Fit Algorithms  
### You classify business risk posture.

“The system probabilistically classifies latent behavioral regimes and quantifies confidence in the organization’s current risk posture.”

You are sensing **risk posture, not fitting models.**

---

## 4. You Do Not Do Feature Engineering  
### You model structural business drivers.

“A latent behavioral factor layer extracts structural drivers of business behavior.”

You are modeling **business structure, not columns.**

---

## 5. You Do Not Monitor Drift  
### You operate an early-warning risk radar.

“The system continuously monitors regime stability and detects silent shifts in underlying risk posture.”

Drift is **risk telemetry — not a model metric.**

---

## 6. You Do Not Create Policies  
### You design Judgment Governance Architecture.

“We formalize decision control, escalation logic, and human accountability.”

You are governing **judgment, not workflows.**

---

## 7. You Do Not Build Dashboards  
### You operate executive telemetry.

“We produce board-grade visibility into regime stability, drift exposure, and escalation states.”

This is **decision nervous system telemetry.**

---

## 8. You Do Not Log Decisions  
### You institutionalize accountability.

“We maintain override and accountability ledgers for regulatory-grade auditability.”

You are building **legal and governance defensibility.**
