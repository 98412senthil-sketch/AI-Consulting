# Override & Accountability Ledger – Regime AI System

## Background
This ledger records all human overrides, manual interventions, and decision deviations made on top of AI-generated recommendations.  
Its purpose is to ensure traceability, accountability, and regulatory auditability for all high-impact AI-assisted decisions.

## Analysis
AI systems provide probabilistic recommendations but cannot own regulatory or ethical responsibility.  
All human overrides must therefore be logged, reviewed, and periodically audited to ensure governance integrity and prevent silent risk accumulation.

## Mandatory Ledger Rules
• All overrides must be logged within 24 hours  
• CRO review required for any R2 override  
• Quarterly audit of ledger entries  
• Repeated overrides trigger model risk review
